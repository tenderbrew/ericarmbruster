Hermes Personal Assistant

Support information — August 25, 2026

Hermes Personal Assistant is a private, single-user, self-hosted Hermes Agent integration. It currently uses read-only Gmail and read-only Google Calendar access to prepare a daily agenda-and-mail brief.

Current Google access

Service Access Purpose
Gmail Read only Review selected mail needed for the daily brief.
Google Calendar Read only Review calendar events needed for the daily brief.

The integration does not currently send, edit, or delete Gmail or Google Calendar data. The completed brief is delivered to a Discord channel chosen by the user.

Support

This is a personal integration rather than a public service. For help with its configuration or access, contact eric.m.armbruster@gmail.com.
