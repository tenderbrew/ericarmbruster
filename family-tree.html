Family tree

Where the family comes from, as far back as the records go. Four streams meet in Pennsylvania: German farmers from Baden, Swiss emigrants from Bern, an Irishman from Donegal, and the coal-country families of Carbon and Luzerne counties. Living relatives are left off this page; the research itself continues.

Third great-grandparents

Armbruster — the emigrants

Eduard Karl Armbruster 1827 – 1906 Born in Kappel am Rhein, Baden — his baptism entry survives in the parish register. Emigrated in 1853; died in Jim Thorpe.

Agatha (Spitzmüller) Armbruster 1835 – 1915 Born in Diersburg, Baden; emigrated December 1852. They raised nine children in the Mauch Chunk area.

Morrison — the Irish line

Thomas Morrison 1818 – 1859 Born in Moville, County Donegal; came to America in 1841. Drowned at the coal chutes on the Lehigh River at Mauch Chunk when his daughter Anna was seventeen months old.

Kappel am Rhein — the old country

The Baden village the Armbrusters farmed for at least 160 years before emigrating. All from Catholic parish registers.

Karl Armbruster b. 1799 & Ursula (Löffel) b. ~1802 “Bürger und Bauer” — citizen and farmer. Married in Kappel in 1821; at least thirteen children.

Valentin Armbruster 1753 – 1820 & Genovefa (Kölble) 1759 – 1818 Married in Kappel in 1783.

Jacob Armbruster 1691 – 1760 & Anna Maria (Richter) 1724 – 1792 The oldest generation found so far — still being verified against the original registers.

Sourced from parish registers in Baden, Pennsylvania death certificates, census records, and newspaper archives. The research is ongoing; corrections welcome at eric.m.armbruster@gmail.com.
