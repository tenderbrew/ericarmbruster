Privacy policy

Hermes Personal Assistant — August 25, 2026

This policy describes how the private, single-user, self-hosted Hermes Personal Assistant integration handles information received through Google APIs.

At a glance

  • Gmail and Google Calendar data are accessed read-only.
  • The OAuth token is stored locally on CT101 with restricted permissions.
  • Only selected data needed for a daily brief is processed by the configured AI model provider.
  • The resulting output is delivered to a Discord channel chosen by the user.

Google data and purpose

Hermes Personal Assistant currently reads selected Gmail messages and Google Calendar events only to prepare a daily agenda-and-mail brief. It does not send, edit, or delete Gmail messages or Calendar events.

The OAuth token is kept locally on CT101 and is limited to the authorized read-only Gmail and Google Calendar permissions.

Processing and delivery

Selected information needed to prepare the brief may be processed by the AI model provider configured for this self-hosted installation. The completed brief is then delivered to the Discord channel chosen by the user.

The integration does not sell, rent, or use Google data for advertising.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Local cron output and session output may be retained on CT101 according to local system retention settings. The user can delete that local output and the locally stored OAuth token at any time.

To revoke Google access, remove this integration from the Google Account's third-party access settings. After revocation, delete the local OAuth token, cron output, and session output from CT101 if those records are no longer wanted. For help, contact eric.m.armbruster@gmail.com.

Google API Limited Use

Hermes Personal Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
