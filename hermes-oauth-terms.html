Hermes Personal Assistant — August 25, 2026

These terms govern personal use of Hermes Personal Assistant, a private, single-user, self-hosted Hermes Agent integration.

Purpose

The integration currently uses read-only Gmail and read-only Google Calendar access to prepare a daily agenda-and-mail brief. The brief is delivered to a Discord channel selected by the user.

Authorization and control

Google authorization is optional and remains under the user's control. The user may limit or revoke the integration's access at any time using Google Account settings, and may remove the local OAuth token and local configuration from CT101.

This is not a public service or a shared product. The user is responsible for the Google Account, Discord channel, AI model provider, local configuration, and scheduling used with the integration.
